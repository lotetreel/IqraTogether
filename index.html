Intent & Tips

Educational prototype inspired by card-based roguelikes. No depiction of sacred figures. Quotations included respectfully as flavor text.

About This Project

This is a small, single-file educational prototype inspired by Slay the Spire and themed around virtues from the Quran and the teachings of the Prophet Muhammad (SAWA) and the Ahlul Bayt. The focus is on learning through mechanics named for virtues such as Sabr (Patience), Sidq (Truthfulness), Dhikr (Remembrance), and Sadaqah (Charity).

Design notes:

  • No depiction of sacred figures or anthropomorphism.
  • Flavor text with brief references is included respectfully.
  • Terminology: Energy is called “Iman”, temporary defense is “Block”.

Sample references (translation paraphrases):
- “Indeed, Allah is with the patient.” (Quran 2:153)
- “The truthful one is at peace.” (Hadith theme on truthfulness)
- “Remember Me; I will remember you.” (Quran 2:152)
Please consult trusted sources/translators for precise wording.

How to play

  • You start each turn with 3 Iman (energy).
  • Draw 5 cards. Click a card to play it if you have enough Iman.
  • Build Block with Sabr to mitigate incoming damage.
  • Press End Turn (Space) to let the enemy act.
  • Defeat the enemy to see a card reward; add one to your deck.

Keyboard: Space to End Turn.
